ProVia Metal Roofing
Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about the durability, efficiency, and long-term value of ProVia’s premium metal roofing systems.

❓ How long does a ProVia metal roof last?

ProVia metal roofs are engineered to last 50+ years with proper installation and minimal maintenance.


❓ Is a metal roof noisy when it rains?

No. Modern metal roofing systems include solid sheathing and insulation layers that significantly reduce sound.


❓ Will a metal roof increase my home’s value?

Yes. Metal roofing is considered a premium upgrade and often increases resale value.


❓ Does ProVia metal roofing help with energy efficiency?

Absolutely. ProVia metal roofs reflect solar heat, reducing cooling costs in summer.


❓ Can a metal roof be installed over my existing shingles?

In many cases, yes. ProVia metal roofing can often be installed over existing shingles.


❓ Is metal roofing safe during lightning storms?

Yes. Metal roofs do not attract lightning and are non-combustible.
